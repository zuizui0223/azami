## Submission remains on scientific HOLD

The figure and manuscript release does not close the six independent gates:

1. adjudicated independent detector boxes;
2. independent orientation, colour and outline reference measurements;
3. botanical calibration of candidate involucre/armature image phenotypes;
4. developmental-stage labels or anthesis-restricted sensitivity;
5. paired flower/background colour control;
6. repeat-photo trait remeasurement and bounded variance decomposition.

These are validation measurements, not reasons to add another environmental raster screen.
