# External completion gates for Azami Chapter 1 v2

Azami Chapter 1 v2 is a complete **present-geography pattern analysis** and a rendered submission candidate. It remains on **scientific HOLD** because the measurements and the two final associations have not yet passed the independent observation-layer tests needed for causal or adaptive interpretation. The computational lane is frozen; additional unregistered trait–environment screening is not a valid way to close these gates.

## What is complete

- 27 continuously defined image endpoints were registered; 22 were measured and five remain explicit unexecuted rows rather than biological zeroes.
- The frozen spatial cohort contains 46,276 observations from 259 source-assigned taxa.
- Below-taxon information loss, current trait geography, all six environmental blocks, within- versus among-taxon associations, sampling-composition sensitivity, broad-space sensitivity and 52-tree historical-placement sensitivity are complete.
- The secondary complete-18 whole-capitulum synthesis is complete and does not replace endpoint-level inference.
- Four main figures, three Supporting Information figures, blinded Main and Supplement DOCX/PDF files, accessibility checks, page-by-page visual QA, a manifest, SHA-256 inventory and a deterministic reviewer ZIP are complete.

## Scientific gates that remain open

1. **Independent detector annotation.** Leakage-free human boxes must establish detection precision, recall and failure modes. Detector-development proposals or pseudo-labels are not independent validation.
2. **Gravity-referenced orientation validation.** The current signed head-axis angle is relative to EXIF image vertical, not gravity. Independent landmarks, camera-roll assessment and preferably three-dimensional orientation are required before interpreting the positive orientation–annual-precipitation association as nodding or rain shielding.
3. **Calibrated colour validation.** Visible CIELAB chroma is not anthocyanin concentration or UV reflectance. Calibration-supported visible/UV reflectance, petal chemistry or pathway measurements are required before interpreting the negative chroma–radiation association as pigment regulation.
4. **Developmental and flowering-season context.** Stage labels or an anthesis-restricted sensitivity are required, and annual precipitation must not be relabelled as rain exposure during flowering.
5. **Flower/background and repeat-photo controls.** Production-scale paired colour controls and repeat-image remeasurement are required to bound illumination, camera, viewpoint and repeated-observation error.
6. **Independent continuous-trait reference measurements.** Orientation, colour, outline and finer surface-image endpoints require endpoint-specific agreement estimates and failure conditions. Unassessable images remain missing evidence, not trait absence.

Failure of an independent validation gate requires a new analysis version and propagation of measurement uncertainty. It must not be repaired by relabelling the current image phenotype or selecting a more favourable environmental model.

## Current claim ceiling

The two final rows are **adaptive-pattern candidates under current sequential controls**:

- taxa from higher-shortwave-radiation environments have lower photographed corolla chroma;
- taxa from wetter environments have a larger EXIF-referenced head-axis angle.

Both retained direction under all 18 applicable sampling perturbations, broad-space control and all 52 historical placements. These results are not demonstrations of plasticity, local adaptation, selection, fitness benefit, rain shielding, anthocyanin synthesis, independent origin or convergence.

## Administrative gates

Author order, affiliations, CRediT roles, acknowledgements, funding, corresponding-author details, repository licence and durable DOI deposit require human completion. The public GitHub repository is not an anonymous review link. The exact reviewer bundle must be copied to an anonymized read-only location before journal submission, and source photographs must remain governed by their individual licence terms.

## Decision

The v2 computational result and presentation package are complete. Submission readiness remains **HOLD pending independent validation and human metadata/licensing**. The next scientific value lies in reference measurements and experimental discrimination, not another post-hoc environmental scan.
