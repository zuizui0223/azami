## Claim boundaries carried into the displays

- Figure 4 is a multidimensional morphospace, not one universal syndrome.
- Figure 5 shows partial phenotypic / measurement-module organization, not validated functional, developmental or genetic modularity.
- Figure 6 separates endpoint evidence grades from the nested multivariate sufficiency tests and does not convert spatial associations into plasticity, adaptation or causation.
- Candidate involucre and armature endpoints remain image-derived C-grade phenotypes pending botanical calibration.
- Historical-placement results remain Supporting Information context and are not resolved phylogenetic correction.

## Excluded or withdrawn displays

- The former negative lability/responsiveness relation and median-split quadrants remain withdrawn.
- Unadjusted or image-quality-unstable candidate rows are not promoted to supported main-text effects.
- The unexecuted `visible_floret_fraction` endpoint is not used for a floral-display conclusion.
