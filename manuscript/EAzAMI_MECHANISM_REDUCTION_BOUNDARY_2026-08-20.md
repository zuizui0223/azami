# Azami → EAzami mechanism-reduction boundary

Status: 2026-08-20

## Decision

`zuizui0223/azami` and `zuizui0223/EAzami` answer different levels of the same research programme.

### Azami = observational macro layer

Azami Chapter 1 freezes what is directly observed from global public-image phenomics:

- continuous head-level orientation, visible colour and outline distributions;
- below-taxon / among-photo / among-head visible variance;
- within-taxon environmental associations;
- spatially explicit environmental structure;
- among-taxon environmental sorting;
- image-derived involucre/spine-like proxy associations.

Its central empirical statement remains observational:

> *Cirsium* capitulum traits show substantial continuous variation below taxon means, and part of that variation is environmentally structured in a trait- and scale-specific way.

Azami does **not** assign those patterns to pollinator selection, antagonist selection, rain protection, phenotypic plasticity, local adaptation or evolutionary rate.

### EAzami = mechanism-reduction layer

EAzami imports a frozen subset of Azami pattern targets and combines them with independent biological-interaction evidence, including:

- quantitative pollinator/display effects;
- quantitative seed-predation/display effects;
- the direct *Cirsium* herbivory seed-output meta-analysis;
- orientation experiments testing pollination and abiotic protection;
- null and scale-dependent interaction results.

EAzami then asks:

> **What is the smallest ecological/evolutionary mechanism family that can generate the joint observation + interaction pattern bundle?**

The canonical EAzami files are:

- `data/evidence/macro_interaction_pattern_targets_v1.csv`;
- `analysis/simulate_macro_interaction_pattern_reduction.py`;
- `data/evidence/macro_interaction_pattern_reduction_result_v1.json`;
- `docs/MACRO_INTERACTION_PATTERN_REDUCTION_PROGRAM.md`;
- `docs/MACRO_INTERACTION_PATTERN_REDUCTION_RESULT_V1.md`.

## Why the simulation does not belong in Chapter 1

Moving the mechanism simulation into Azami would blur a critical evidence boundary:

`observed environment–trait structure ≠ demonstrated causal mechanism`.

The simulation deliberately uses evidence not generated by Chapter 1, including pollination, herbivory and seed-output experiments. It therefore belongs downstream in EAzami.

Azami supplies **patterns to explain**. EAzami supplies **candidate mechanisms and reduction tests**.

## Frozen handoff targets from Azami

The first mechanism-reduction screen uses, among others:

- below-assigned-taxon visible variance: 0.589–0.931 across nine endpoints;
- orientation × BIO1 positive association;
- corolla chroma × BIO12 positive association;
- aspect ratio × BIO4 positive and × BIO12 negative associations;
- temperature-seasonality associations of involucre roughness/spread/spine-like projection proxies;
- near-zero precision-aware common-lability relationship (`rho ≈ -0.057`);
- stronger global environmental structure for orientation/visible colour than gross outline.

These values remain owned by Azami and must not be silently refit or changed inside EAzami.

## Interpretation rule

A successful EAzami simulation does not retrospectively turn an Azami association into causal evidence.

Instead:

1. Azami identifies the empirical pattern;
2. interaction literature and EAzami meta-analysis constrain plausible mechanisms;
3. simulation asks which mechanism families are structurally capable of reproducing the joint pattern bundle;
4. ancestry-resolved field experiments test the remaining discriminating mechanisms.

This keeps the global macro paper submission-focused while allowing the doctoral programme to pursue mechanistic reduction without duplicating or overwriting Chapter 1.
