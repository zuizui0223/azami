# Main figure captions

**Figure 1. From public photographs to continuous multiscale thistle phenomics.** Open-licensed photographs from the global *Cirsium* and allied Cardueae atlas illustrate the measurement route. An object detector localizes visible capitula; examples show established orientation, visible-colour and outline measurements together with finer continuous involucral and armature geometry from the expanded GEB v2 contract. Repeated image-derived phenotypes support nested visible-variance analysis, taxon morphospace, within-taxon environmental associations, matched among-taxon comparisons and whole-capitulum organization. The examples illustrate workflow and measurement scope and are not independent accuracy tests.

**Figure 2. Geographic sampling and analytical domain of global *Cirsium* and allied Cardueae photographs.** (a) Density per 1° cell among detector-positive observations with finite coordinates. (b) One-degree display-cell density for 46,276 observations from 259 taxa in the primary taxon × 0.25° spatially thinned cohort. (c) Complementary balanced-atlas and exhaustive/spatially thinned data streams, including the complete-18 cohort used for multilevel phenotype-space analysis. (d) Occupied environmental domain under CHELSA 1981–2010 normals. Maps use a Mollweide equal-area projection and diagnose sampling of public photographs, not global representativeness of all thistles.

**Figure 3. Nested decomposition of visible capitulum-trait variance in the global thistle atlas.** For each of nine established primary continuous endpoints, visible image sums of squares are partitioned among source-assigned taxon means, among photographs within taxa and among detected heads within photographs. The combined below-taxon fraction ranges from 0.589 to 0.931. Components describe visible image phenotypes, not genetic variance; Figure S1.1 presents one-head-per-photograph and equal-replication sensitivities.

**Figure 4. Continuous capitulum traits occupy a multidimensional present-state geography.** The full 27-endpoint inventory shows 22 measured and five unexecuted endpoints, with missingness retained as unassessable rather than biological absence. Across measured endpoints, 81.4-98.6% of raw observation-level variation lies below taxon means, and equal-two-per-taxon medians remain 28.7-58.5%. Endpoint coverage and geographic distributions are shown for orientation, visible colour, gross outline, involucral architecture and armature-like image geometry. The accompanying morphospace contains 127 taxa complete for all 18 measured inferential endpoints; PC1-PC3 explain 42.28% cumulatively. PCA summarizes multidimensionality but does not replace endpoint-level distributions.

**Figure 5. Trait-environment alignment depends on both the continuous endpoint and the spatial scale.** The complete atlas retains all 26 inferential units against BIO1, BIO4, BIO12, BIO15, radiation, VPD, wind, growing-season precipitation and potential NPP; historical tiers do not select or hide rows. In the primary min5 comparison, seven of 234 rows are supported at both scales, 18 within taxa only, three among taxa only, 152 at neither scale and 54 are not comparable. Orientation aligns with BIO1 within taxa and BIO12 among taxa, while most supported involucral and surface-proxy rows are within-only. Unsupported and unexecuted rows remain visible. These are cross-sectional associations, not plasticity, adaptation or direct causation.

**Figure 6. Sampling-composition, spatial and historical gates define the EAzami I-IV handoff.** The retrospective composition audit evaluates all 36 q-supported rows under 674 declared scenarios without recomputing significance: 16 of 26 within-taxon rows and all ten among-taxon rows retain direction throughout. Of the five within-taxon and two among-taxon rows that pass the v2-native broad-space gate, four and two are also composition-stable; projection roughness by radiation is the one composition-sensitive spatial survivor. Joint hue remains spatially structured and does not pass. The two among-taxon survivors—lower chroma with higher radiation and a larger signed head-axis angle relative to EXIF-image vertical with higher annual precipitation—retain direction in all 18 applicable composition scenarios and retain direction and support on all 52 placement trees, so they are labelled adaptive-pattern candidates under current controls. A secondary panel shows that the process extension adds no supported whole-capitulum information beyond the core four within taxa but does among taxa. Azami passes definition, uncertainty, distribution, geography, environmental association, scale class and measurement/q/spatial/composition/historical gate status to EAzami-I function validation, II repeated nuclear history, III origin-class discrimination and IV convergence/adaptive-convergence tests. No panel establishes representative sampling, adaptation, direct mechanism, resolved ancestry or convergence.
