# Main figure captions

**Figure 1. From public photographs to continuous multiscale thistle phenomics.** Open-licensed photographs from the global *Cirsium* and allied Cardueae atlas illustrate the measurement route. An object detector localizes visible capitula; examples show established orientation, visible-colour and outline measurements together with finer continuous involucral and armature geometry from the expanded GEB v2 contract. Repeated image-derived phenotypes support nested visible-variance analysis, taxon morphospace, within-taxon environmental associations, matched among-taxon comparisons and whole-capitulum organization. The examples illustrate workflow and measurement scope and are not independent accuracy tests.

**Figure 2. Geographic sampling and analytical domain of global *Cirsium* and allied Cardueae photographs.** (a) Density per 1° cell among detector-positive observations with finite coordinates. (b) One-degree display-cell density for 46,276 observations from 259 taxa in the primary taxon × 0.25° spatially thinned cohort. (c) Complementary balanced-atlas and exhaustive/spatially thinned data streams, including the complete-18 cohort used for multilevel phenotype-space analysis. (d) Occupied environmental domain under CHELSA 1981–2010 normals. Maps use a Mollweide equal-area projection and diagnose sampling of public photographs, not global representativeness of all thistles.

**Figure 3. Nested decomposition of visible capitulum-trait variance in the global thistle atlas.** For each of nine established primary continuous endpoints, visible image sums of squares are partitioned among source-assigned taxon means, among photographs within taxa and among detected heads within photographs. The combined below-taxon fraction ranges from 0.589 to 0.931. Components describe visible image phenotypes, not genetic variance; Figure S1.1 presents one-head-per-photograph and equal-replication sensitivities.

**Figure 4. Expanded taxon-level capitulum morphospace is multidimensional.** (a, b) Scores of the 127 taxa complete for all 18 measured inferential endpoints on PC1–PC2 and PC1–PC3, with standardized loading vectors coloured by registered measurement module. (c) PC1, PC2 and PC3 explain 18.49%, 12.01% and 11.78% of variance, respectively (42.28% cumulative). (d) Endpoint loadings on the first three axes. The frozen nine-primary-endpoint PCA is retained in Figure S1.10. No principal axis is interpreted as a single biological syndrome.

**Figure 5. The whole-capitulum phenotype is partially organized and its geometry changes across biological scales.** (a) Within-taxon 17-unit association matrix derived from taxon-centred observations with equal total taxon weight. (b) Among-taxon association matrix derived from taxon medians. Registered measurement-module boundaries are shown without implying validated functional or genetic modules. (c) Within-module minus between-module contrasts and taxon-bootstrap intervals for the main ≥5-observation scope and the ≥2-observation sensitivity. In the main scope, contrasts are 0.164502 within taxa and 0.088475 among taxa. (d) Pairwise within- versus among-taxon association strengths; the two upper-triangle geometries have Spearman similarity 0.366299 (bootstrap interval 0.118457–0.399817). The result supports partial phenotypic / measurement-module organization and incomplete cross-scale correspondence.

**Figure 6. Environmental organization is endpoint specific, scale specific and dependent on environmental representation.** (a) Counts in the frozen eight-grade evidence hierarchy, with the two quality-robust and one image-sensitive C-grade candidate rows identified; complete row-level details are in Table S1.22. (b) Matched classification of 68 endpoint × BIO1/BIO4/BIO12/BIO15 rows as supported at both scales, within taxa only, among taxa only or neither. Orientation aligns with BIO1 within taxa and BIO12 among taxa, and all three final candidate rows are within only. (c) Partial R² for the five-variable process extension and growing-season precipitation beyond the four-variable core at the ≥5 and ≥2 replication thresholds. The process extension is unsupported within taxa but supported among taxa (main partial R² = 0.214978, P = 0.000700); growing-season precipitation is the only block-specific increment supported at both thresholds. Evidence grades and nested tests describe cross-sectional spatial structure, not plasticity, adaptation or direct environmental causation.
