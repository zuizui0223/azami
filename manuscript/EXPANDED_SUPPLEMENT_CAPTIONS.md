# Expanded Supporting Information captions

**Figure S1.10. Established and expanded taxon morphospaces.** (a) Taxon scores and standardized loadings for the nine established primary endpoints, reconstructed with the same complete-case, population-standardized singular-value decomposition used by the frozen GEB v2 geography workflow. (b) Corresponding PC1–PC2 representation for the 18 measured inferential endpoints. (c) Explained variance of PC1–PC3. The comparison is descriptive and does not redefine either frozen inferential cohort.

**Figure S1.11. Module-specific taxon morphospaces.** PC1–PC2 scores for visible colour, outline shape, involucre architecture and armature, using the module-specific complete-taxon scopes in the frozen trait-geography artifact. Registered modules are measurement groupings rather than validated functional or genetic modules.

**Figure S1.12. Full candidate image-quality sensitivity audit.** (a) Quality-adjusted standardized coefficients for nine measured candidate endpoints × four CHELSA predictors; asterisks denote candidate-quality-family Benjamini–Hochberg q < 0.05. (b) Resolution-stratum interpretation, separating quality-robust, image-sensitive or uncertain, and quality-adjusted but not FDR-supported rows. This panel does not replace the registry-wide candidate screen or botanical calibration.

**Figure S1.13. Full matched 68-row coefficient and support map.** Standardized coefficients within taxa and among taxa for 17 inferential units × BIO1, BIO4, BIO12 and BIO15; hue is shown as circular effect magnitude rather than a signed linear coefficient. Asterisks denote the frozen scale-specific Benjamini–Hochberg decision.

**Figure S1.14. Whole-capitulum organization under the ≥2-observation sensitivity.** Within- and among-taxon 17-unit association matrices and pairwise cross-scale correspondence for the expanded 75-taxon sensitivity scope. The module-contrast panel also retains the main ≥5-observation estimates for direct comparison.

**Figure S1.15. Stand-alone environmental-block multivariate R².** Six predeclared environmental blocks across within- and among-taxon scales and the ≥5 and ≥2 scopes. Daggers identify nominal permutation P < 0.05; no block passes the stand-alone block-family Benjamini–Hochberg rule. These tests are distinct from the nested increments in Figure S1.16.

**Figure S1.16. Full nested increments beyond BIO1, BIO4, BIO12 and BIO15.** Partial R² and Freedman–Lane permutation P values for the omnibus five-variable process extension and four block-specific extensions at both scales and replication thresholds. Asterisks denote the predeclared nested support rule.

**Figure S1.17. Cross-scale coefficient-matrix cosine sensitivity.** Bootstrap intervals for within- versus among-taxon coefficient-matrix cosine similarity for six environmental blocks at both replication thresholds. Every interval includes zero; coefficient rotation is therefore descriptive and inconclusive.
