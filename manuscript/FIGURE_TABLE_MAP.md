This file records the submission-facing relationship among the main manuscript, Appendix S1 and the repository assets from which figures and tables are built. The final Chapter 1 narrative requires **six main figures and two main tables**. Figures 1–3 retain the established workflow, geography and nested-variance layers. Figures 4–6 now follow the PR #71 unit of inference: continuous trait, environmental gradient and spatial scale. Whole-capitulum organization is a secondary synthesis in Appendix S1.

## Main figures

1. **Figure 1 — image to multiscale analysis:** open-licensed photographs → capitulum localization → established and expanded continuous measurements → repeated numerical phenotypes → nested variance, morphospace, within/among environmental comparison and whole-capitulum organization.
2. **Figure 2 — geographic sampling and analytical domain:** detector-positive observations, the 46,276-observation primary cohort, balanced/exhaustive streams, expanded measurement intersections, the complete-18 cohort and CHELSA environmental domain.
3. **Figure 3 — nested visible variance:** source-assigned taxon → photograph → head decomposition across the nine established primary endpoints. This is the frozen demonstration that taxon means discard substantial repeated visible variation.
4. **Figure 4 — trait-specific present-state atlas:** coverage and geographic distributions across the expanded continuous-trait contract, accompanied by the 127-taxon, 18-endpoint morphospace. PC1–PC3 explain 18.49%, 12.01% and 11.78%; PCA is a multidimensionality summary, not a replacement for endpoint-level geography.
5. **Figure 5 — trait x environmental gradient x scale:** the complete 234-row full-27/full-environment status atlas, including unsupported and unexecuted rows, and the matched within/among support classification. The figure shows that orientation aligns with different climatic axes within and among taxa and that most supported involucral/surface rows are within-only.
6. **Figure 6 — sampling/spatial/historical gates and EAzami I-IV handoff:** 16/26 within and 10/10 among selected directions stable across sampling perturbations; five within and two among broad-space passes; the one sampling-sensitive within spatial pass; the two among pairs retained across all sampling scenarios and 52 placement trees; and the exact bundle passed to I function validation, II repeated nuclear history, III origin-class discrimination and IV convergence/adaptive-convergence tests.

The previous whole-capitulum-centred plan is superseded because it would reassemble decomposed continuous traits before their distinct geographies and environmental hypotheses are shown. The whole-capitulum matrices remain an important synthesis and are retained in Table S1.19 and Figure S1.14.
