# Submission figure, table and supplement map

This file records the submission-facing relationship among the main manuscript, Appendix S1 and the repository assets from which figures and tables are built. The final Chapter 1 narrative requires **six main figures and two main tables**. Figures 1–3 retain the established workflow, geography and nested-variance layers. Figures 4–6 present the expanded morphospace, whole-capitulum organization and scale-dependent environmental result. Historical-placement detail remains in Appendix S1.

## Main figures

1. **Figure 1 — image to multiscale analysis:** open-licensed photographs → capitulum localization → established and expanded continuous measurements → repeated numerical phenotypes → nested variance, morphospace, within/among environmental comparison and whole-capitulum organization.
2. **Figure 2 — geographic sampling and analytical domain:** detector-positive observations, the 46,276-observation primary cohort, balanced/exhaustive streams, expanded measurement intersections, the complete-18 cohort and CHELSA environmental domain.
3. **Figure 3 — nested visible variance:** source-assigned taxon → photograph → head decomposition across the nine established primary endpoints. This is the frozen demonstration that taxon means discard substantial repeated visible variation.
4. **Figure 4 — expanded taxon morphospace:** 127 taxa complete for all 18 measured inferential endpoints, with standardized loadings and PC1–PC3 variance of 18.49%, 12.01% and 11.78%. The purpose is to show a multidimensional phenotype rather than one syndrome.
5. **Figure 5 — multilevel whole-capitulum organization:** within- and among-taxon 17-unit association matrices, registered-module boundaries, module contrasts and cross-scale matrix similarity. This figure distinguishes partial phenotypic / measurement-module organization from validated functional or genetic modularity.
6. **Figure 6 — environmental evidence and sufficiency across scales:** endpoint-level A/B/C evidence atlas, 68-row within/among support classification and nested BIO1/BIO4/BIO12/BIO15 versus process-extension comparison. The figure emphasizes scale-specific environmental organization and the stable among-taxon growing-season-precipitation increment.

The previous five-figure plan is superseded because combining whole-capitulum organization and the endpoint/environment evidence atlas in one panel would conceal the central scale-geometry result.
