# Azami Chapter 1 canonical spatial-scale mainline

## Decision

Chapter 1 starts from the complete GEB v2 measurement design, not from the old nine-endpoint v1 result set. GEB v2 did not merely add endpoints. It changed the unit of evidence from a categorical or taxon-mean database record to repeated, coordinate-bearing continuous measurements and enlarged the environmental hypothesis space to six literature-motivated blocks.

The canonical line is:

> photograph -> 27 registered continuous endpoints -> variation hidden by taxon means -> present-day trait geography -> six environmental-hypothesis blocks -> within- versus among-taxon correspondence -> sampling-composition + spatial sensitivity -> historical-placement sensitivity -> adaptive-pattern candidates -> EAzami I-IV

The unit of inference is therefore a **continuous trait x environmental gradient x biological scale** row whose measurement, q, sampling-composition, spatial and historical statuses remain visible. The 27 registered endpoints are the starting inventory. An unexecuted endpoint remains an explicit missing-evidence row; it is not silently removed. A whole-capitulum summary is optional supplementary synthesis and never replaces the decomposed endpoints.

The new `full27/full-environment` lane is versioned separately from frozen earlier analyses. It does not overwrite them, use their nine-endpoint selection as an entry rule or use A/B/C evidence bins to hide rows.

## Chapter 1 question

Azami asks:

> What continuous capitulum variation can coordinate-bearing photographs recover beyond categorical and taxon-mean databases, how is that variation geographically organized, and which trait-environment associations persist across biological scale, uneven sampling composition, spatial structure and current historical-placement uncertainty?

This question has four linked parts:

1. **Measurement contribution:** how can photographs be converted into a 27-endpoint category-free continuous-trait inventory, and which endpoints were actually measurable?
2. **Information-loss contribution:** how much observation-level visible variation lies below taxon means and would therefore be compressed by a one-row-per-species trait database?
3. **Geographic contribution:** because each photograph retains coordinates, where is each measured endpoint observed and how broad is its spatial support?
4. **Environmental contribution:** which of six literature-motivated blocks—thermal, hydric, radiative/atmospheric, growing-season water input, mechanical and resource/productivity—aligns with each endpoint?
5. **Scale contribution:** does the endpoint-gradient relationship occur within taxa, among taxa, at both scales or at neither?
6. **Robustness and candidate gate:** which q-supported rows retain direction under dominant-taxon, equal-taxon-weight, broad-region and native-only perturbations, and which among-taxon relationships additionally pass the v2-native spatial screen and audited historical-placement trees?

The current phylogenetic layer is a placement sensitivity, not a resolved partition of environmental, spatial and phylogenetic effects. “Adaptive-pattern candidate” means that a present association was not readily removed by the tested controls. It does not demonstrate heritability, selection, fitness benefit, causal mechanism or adaptive evolution.

## Scale ladder

| Level | Azami estimand | Current role |
|---|---|---|
| Head within photograph | repeated visible phenotype | separates head-level heterogeneity from taxon summaries |
| Photograph within taxon | observation-level visible phenotype | preserves geography and image-level replication |
| Within-taxon geography | taxon-centred trait-environment association | primary present-state spatial gradient |
| Among-taxon geography | taxon-median environmental sorting and matched association | tests whether taxon turnover reproduces the within-taxon pattern |
| Global sampled domain | trait coverage, geography and environmental occupancy | defines scope; not proof of worldwide representativeness |
| Historical sensitivity | randomized placement on an incomplete backbone | bounds interpretation; does not resolve ancestry |

## Trait and environmental-hypothesis architecture

The expanded 27-endpoint contract is the starting universe and is presented as separable continuous axes rather than one larger morphology score:

- orientation;
- visible colour, with circular hue treated jointly;
- gross outline;
- involucral architecture;
- armature-like or projection image geometry requiring botanical calibration.

The environmental architecture is likewise explicit and exhaustive for this lane:

- **thermal:** annual mean temperature and temperature seasonality;
- **hydric:** annual precipitation and precipitation seasonality;
- **radiative/atmospheric:** mean shortwave radiation and mean vapour-pressure deficit;
- **growing-season water input:** climatic growing-season precipitation;
- **mechanical:** mean near-surface wind;
- **resource/productivity:** modelled potential net primary productivity.

Growing-season precipitation is not taxon-specific flowering-season rainfall, and potential NPP is not local measured resource supply. The blocks encode prior ecological hypotheses; they are not observed causal mechanisms at a capitulum.

Adding endpoints does not authorize an uncontracted all-raster screen. Unsupported trait-gradient pairs remain part of the result because they show that capitulum diversity is selectively, not universally, environmentally organized.

## Evidence hierarchy

The Chapter 1 headline order is:

1. photographs can yield registered, reproducible continuous endpoints rather than only categories;
2. repeated measurements recover visible diversity hidden by taxon means;
3. coordinates turn the trait table into an endpoint-specific present-geography atlas;
4. environmental alignment is selective across six hypothesis blocks rather than one universal syndrome;
5. the same endpoint need not align with the same gradient within and among taxa;
6. sampling-composition, spatial and historical-placement sensitivities annotate fragility and separate descriptive associations from adaptive-pattern candidates without deleting the complete atlas.

The final whole-capitulum result is still biologically useful. It rejects a universal syndrome and demonstrates partial organization, but its complete-case cohort is narrower than the primary global trait universe and it must not reassemble continuously decomposed traits into a single causal object.

## Realized result of the full-27 / full-environment lane

The analysis retained the full 27-endpoint inventory. Twenty-two endpoints had finite measurements and five remained explicit unexecuted rows; hue sine and cosine formed one joint circular unit. Across the 22 measured endpoints, 81.4-98.6% of raw observation-level variation lay below source-assigned taxon means. After equalizing replication to two observations per eligible taxon, the endpoint medians were still 28.7-58.5%. This is the direct evidence for the taxon-mean information-loss claim.

All 46,276 coordinate-bearing observations were joined to the nine frozen environmental variables. Eight variables had complete coverage and wind had 99.989% coverage. The primary min5 comparison classified seven endpoint-gradient pairs as supported at both scales, 18 as within-taxon only, three as among-taxon only, 152 as neither and 54 as not comparable because measurement was absent or had no finite variation.

The sampling-composition audit evaluated 26 within-taxon and ten among-taxon q-supported pairs under 674 perturbations. All ten among-taxon directions were stable. Sixteen within-taxon directions were stable throughout, whereas ten reversed under equal taxon weighting, dominant-taxon omission, broad-region omission or native-only restriction. This explicitly shows that global geographic extent is not global representativeness.

The broad-space gate retained five within-taxon patterns: orientation-BIO1, chroma-VPD, involucre length/width-BIO15, projection roughness-rsds and surface high-frequency energy-BIO1. Four also retained direction throughout the sampling-composition audit. Projection roughness-rsds reversed under equal taxon weighting and joint omission of the two most observed taxa, so it remains visible as a spatial pass with a sampling-sensitive annotation. None is demonstrated plasticity or adaptation.

Only two among-taxon pairs passed the broad-space gate and all 52 audited historical-placement trees:

1. higher shortwave radiation aligned with lower corolla chroma;
2. higher annual precipitation aligned with a larger signed head-axis angle relative to EXIF-image vertical.

They are labelled **adaptive-pattern candidates under current controls** and both retained direction in every applicable sampling-composition scenario. The sampling audit is retrospective, while the candidate controls remain sequential broad-space and historical-placement sensitivities rather than a resolved joint causal model. The exact result ledger is `manuscript/results/GEB_V2_FULL27_FULL_ENVIRONMENT_RESULTS_2026-08-27.md`.

## Main-figure logic

1. **Measurement universe:** photograph -> detected head -> 27 registered continuous endpoints -> measured/unexecuted status.
2. **Information recovered:** below-taxon visible variance and equal-replication sensitivity, showing what taxon means compress.
3. **Present geography:** global observation support and endpoint-specific maps made possible by retained coordinates.
4. **Six-block environmental atlas:** every endpoint-gradient status, including unsupported and unexecuted rows.
5. **Trait x gradient x scale:** matched within-taxon and among-taxon results without tier-based row deletion.
6. **Candidate gate and handoff:** v2-native spatial sensitivity, historical-placement sensitivity and the exact candidate/non-candidate reason passed to EAzami.

Whole-capitulum association matrices move to the expanded Supplement unless a final layout can retain them as a small synthesis panel without displacing the trait-gradient result.

## Azami to EAzami handoff

Azami freezes a present-state target bundle for each eligible continuous trait:

> trait definition + measurement uncertainty + present distribution + geographic support + associated environmental gradient + within/among scale class + measurement/q/sampling-composition/spatial/historical gate status

EAzami then advances through four evidence-gated series:

1. **EAzami-I — functional annotation and validation:** annotate each component with candidate functions, then test measurement identity and trait -> function/performance links. A literature annotation is not yet a validated functional trait.
2. **EAzami-II — repeated history on a nuclear ancestry set:** reconstruct when each admitted functional trait state or continuous regime arose and how many transitions or reoccupations are supported across the accepted nuclear-tree/network uncertainty set.
3. **EAzami-III — historical-class discrimination:** distinguish phylogenetic retention, independent origin, ancestral polymorphism/incomplete lineage sorting and introgression/hybrid history. Repeated tip similarity or a parsimony count alone does not establish independent origins.
4. **EAzami-IV — convergence and adaptive convergence:** promote independent origins with the same validated function to functional convergence. Promote to adaptive convergence only when comparable selection pressure and a trait -> function -> reproductive-fitness path are supported independently.

Continuous Azami measurements must not be converted post hoc into convenient EAzami categories. Any functional annotation, state threshold, transition model, tree/network set or convergence statistic requires a declared EAzami contract. Functional annotation cannot choose a preferred history, and historical reconstruction cannot retroactively turn an Azami spatial association into plasticity, adaptation or causal mechanism.

## One-line doctoral boundary

> **Azami identifies what continuous capitulum states exist now, where they occur and which environmental gradients they align with; EAzami validates their candidate functions, reconstructs repeated history on nuclear ancestry, distinguishes independent origins from retained or reticulate history, and only then tests convergence or adaptive convergence.**
