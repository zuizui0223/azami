# Chapter 1 final manuscript strategy

## Primary claim

The Chapter 1 unit is **continuous trait x environmental gradient x spatial scale**, not a taxon mean, a categorical morphology or one whole-capitulum syndrome.

## Why this is the PR #71 recovery

GEB v2 expanded two linked hypothesis spaces:

1. the phenotype space, through the 27-endpoint continuous contract; and
2. the environmental space, through trait-specific core gradients, contracted spatial context and a predeclared process extension.

The manuscript therefore leads with which trait aligns with which gradient and whether the result occurs within taxa, among taxa or both. Whole-capitulum covariance remains a useful synthesis, but it follows rather than organizes those results.

## Headline result order

1. Repeated photographs preserve continuous diversity hidden by taxon means.
2. Trait-specific environmental associations are selective, graded and not interchangeable.
3. The same endpoint may align with different gradients within and among taxa.
4. The environmental representation sufficient within taxa is incomplete among taxa.
5. A multidimensional, partially organized whole phenotype rejects one universal syndrome.
6. Spatial robustness is stronger than the current historical resolution; the latter remains a sensitivity, not a resolved partition.

## Azami and EAzami

Azami outputs:

> trait definition + uncertainty + present distribution + geographic support + environmental association + scale class + measurement/q/sampling-composition/spatial/historical gate status

EAzami advances through:

> I candidate-function validation + II repeated history on nuclear ancestry + III retention/independent-origin/ancestral-polymorphism/introgression discrimination + IV functional convergence and adaptive convergence

The temporal model must use declared continuous models or predeclared state thresholds. Repeated similarity is not independent origin; adaptive convergence additionally requires comparable selection pressure and reproductive-fitness evidence. No EAzami series can turn spatial association itself into causal mechanism.

## Figure strategy

Figures 1–3 establish measurement, domain and nested variation. Figure 4 shows the trait-specific present-state atlas; Figure 5 is the trait-gradient-scale evidence map; Figure 6 separates spatial robustness, environmental sufficiency and unresolved history before displaying the EAzami handoff. Whole-capitulum matrices are Supporting Information by default.

## Claim ceiling

Chapter 1 establishes present spatial patterns. It does not establish measurement validity before external calibration, biological sources of all below-taxon variance, plasticity, adaptation, pollinator or antagonist causation, defensive function, direct environmental mechanism, resolved ancestry or evolutionary rate.
