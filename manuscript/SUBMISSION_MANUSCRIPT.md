## Why thistles

Thistles are the comparative model system, not the genus-specific endpoint of the paper. The Carduus–Cirsium group combines a repeatedly photographed complex capitulum with large visible disparity across orientation, colour, outline and involucral architecture. It also contains geographically replicated rapid regional radiations, including separate Pleistocene radiations in Japan and North America. That combination makes the group a useful stress test for whether repeated phenotypic diversification across distinct regional lineages is compressed by categorical states or one taxon mean.

The manuscript does not promote these regional radiations to demonstrated adaptive radiations by assumption. Current Azami spatial associations also do not establish repeated origins or convergence. They define the continuous trait axes that downstream function and nuclear-history analyses can test across the replicated radiations.

## Main versus Supporting Information

The Main manuscript contains the inferential narrative required to evaluate the general result:

- continuous trait recovery from public photographs;
- information compressed by taxon means;
- endpoint-specific within/among scale structure;
- the two final candidate patterns under the declared sequential gates.

Supporting Information is audit-oriented rather than a duplicate manuscript. It contains the complete endpoint inventory, coverage, full supported/unsupported model grids, all 674 sampling perturbations, broad-space and residual-Moran diagnostics, historical-placement tables and external validation gates. Supporting Figures S1–S3 are distinct diagnostics for endpoint coverage, the full selected-row sampling audit and the complete broad-space diagnostic surface; Main Figures 2–4 are not repeated as Supplement figures.

The older nine-endpoint selection, lability/quadrant story and PR69-era endpoint hierarchy are not active manuscript results. Their recovery point is Git history and the immutable scientific tag `azami-ch1-v2-2026-08-27`; they are not duplicated in the submission-facing tree.

The paper remains on scientific HOLD for independent detector and trait-reference validation, developmental-stage control, paired colour calibration and repeat-photo remeasurement. The attached Word/PDF files are therefore submission candidates, not a claim that these external gates have closed.
