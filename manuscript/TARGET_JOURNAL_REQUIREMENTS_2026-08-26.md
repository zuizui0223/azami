# Target-journal requirements and current compliance

**Checked:** 2026-08-26
**Repository state assessed:** current `manuscript/` sources; five main figures, two main tables, a GEB-style structured abstract, and **SUBMISSION HOLD**.

## Bottom line

For the manuscript in its current empirical form, **Global Ecology and Biogeography (GEB) is the closest formal and scope fit**, conditional on clearing the scientific HOLD. Its Research Article format matches the current approximately 281-word seven-heading abstract, approximately 5,103-word main text, 46-item reference list and seven display pieces. **Ecography is a credible second target**, but the current main text is approximately 100 words over its 5,000-word maximum, the seven display pieces exceed its hard maximum of six and its Supporting Information citation convention would require revision. **Methods in Ecology and Evolution (MEE) is not a good current-form target**: the journal requires the method rather than the empirical findings to be the central advance, broad applicability beyond one taxon/system, and normally simulation or benchmark testing for computational methods; it also states that workflows linking existing methods generally are not new methods.

None of the three targets is submission-ready from the present repository. The common blocking issues are the unavailable anonymized peer-review data/code package, absence of a durable DOI archive and selected repository/code licence, missing separate title-page/administrative material, and unresolved scientific validation gates already recorded by the project. These are consistent with, rather than additional to, the repository's current **SUBMISSION HOLD**.

## Target-specific decision

### 1. Global Ecology and Biogeography

**Conditional first choice after the scientific gates close.** The geographic and taxonomic extent, macroecological analyses, exact abstract structure and current seven display pieces align unusually well with the formal requirements. The main editorial risk is not format but whether the paper's deliberately bounded conclusion is a sufficiently general conceptual advance beyond *Cirsium* and allied Cardueae. The abstract, Figure 1 and Figure 5 should therefore foreground the general lesson about preserving repeated visible phenotypes and explicitly auditing image-derived inference, while retaining the current non-causal claim ceiling.

Before upload: clear the scientific HOLD; preserve the current keyword line and running title under 40 characters; prepare the separate anonymous main file and identifying title page; make data/code anonymously reviewable; select licences and a durable archive; prepare the required cover letter; and verify every rendered map and Word metadata field.

### 2. Ecography

**Plausible second choice after reformatting.** The global spatial sampling, climate associations and explicit sampling-domain maps fit the journal's spatial ecology/macroecology remit. Reduce the main displays from seven to six (the least costly change is moving one of the two main descriptive tables to Supporting Information), replace the GEB-specific abstract headings if desired, expand “BH”, add keywords and a significance statement, and convert the manuscript's item-by-item Supporting Information citations to Ecography's general convention. A documented colour-vision and contrast audit is required before treating the figure package as ready.

### 3. Methods in Ecology and Evolution

**Do not submit the current manuscript unchanged.** Formal length is acceptable, but the present paper leads with biological patterns recovered using an image-analysis workflow. To become an MEE Research Article it would need a genuinely new, broadly transferable method as the headline; simulation/benchmark evidence; independent detector and trait-measurement validation; demonstrations across taxa or systems; a four-part numbered abstract; and a fully licensed, reviewer-runnable code package. These are substantive scientific changes, not a formatting conversion. A shorter Application/Workflow route would likewise require a reusable open-source software product and would not match the present 7,000-word empirical manuscript.
