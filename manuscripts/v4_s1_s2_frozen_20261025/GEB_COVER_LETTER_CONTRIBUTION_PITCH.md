# Working contribution paragraph for a future GEB cover letter

**Status:** editorial draft only; no submission, correspondence, author identity, priority claim or journal acceptance implied. This contribution statement is deliberately **separate from the scientific Results/Discussion**.

> We would like to submit a Research Article provisionally entitled “Global photographic phenomics reveals precipitation-associated capitulum presentation and floral colour gradients in *Cirsium*” to *Global Ecology and Biogeography*. Global trait databases and categorical descriptions give necessary coverage but obscure repeated, continuous variation in the same reproductive structure. *Cirsium* offers an unusually informative comparative system: distinct regional radiations, contrasting climatic settings and extensive public photographs of capitula with diverse presentation, floral colour and outline. We source-audit these images and their continuous trait measurements rather than assuming one representative value per species. Across 178 native taxa, the image-referenced angle of capitula was positively associated with warm-quarter precipitation after Holm correction; the observed geographic direction is compatible with, but does not prove, rain avoidance. Within two well-sampled *Cirsium* species, separate native/introduced-range models identified three Holm-supported introduced-range colour–environment gradients related to radiation or elevation, whose native estimates clarify that differences in adjusted significance are not necessarily evolutionary differences. The study joins global photographic phenotyping with a biogeographically motivated conspecific comparison and an explicitly bounded ecological interpretation. Original matched-photograph covariance analyses are retained as exploratory supporting evidence, not a claim of genetic modularity or adaptive trait combinations.
