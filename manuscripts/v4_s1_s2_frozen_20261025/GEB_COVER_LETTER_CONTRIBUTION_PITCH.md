# Working contribution paragraph for a future GEB cover letter

**Status:** editorial draft only; no submission, correspondence, author identity, priority claim or journal acceptance implied. This contribution statement is deliberately **separate from the scientific Results/Discussion**.

> We would like to submit our Research Article on the environmental organisation of globally photographed *Cirsium* capitulum traits to *Global Ecology and Biogeography*. Existing species-level trait databases and coarse floral categories provide valuable geographic coverage but rarely preserve repeated, continuous measurements and the covariance among multiple parts of the same floral display. Our study combines source-audited public-image phenotypes of capitulum presentation, colour and outline with three linked ecological questions: partial integration and alternative configurations of multiple traits within shared photographs; the association of image-referenced capitulum presentation with warm-quarter precipitation among native taxa; and comparable within-stratum colour–environment relationships of two source-qualified species across native and introduced ranges. In the frozen analysis, an image-referenced angle–warm-quarter precipitation association survives correction among native taxa, while radiation/elevation-related colour associations are detected in introduced strata and are interpreted alongside their native counterparts. Trait associations are stronger within predefined visible domains, yet multiple configurations coexist within taxa. The approach yields interpretable macroecological hypotheses while retaining explicit sampling, multiple-testing and photographic-measurement limits. We believe this combination of quantitative phenotyping, biogeographic comparison and trait-organisation analysis will be of interest to the journal's readers.
