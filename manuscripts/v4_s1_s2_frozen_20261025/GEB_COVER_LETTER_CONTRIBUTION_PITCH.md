# Working contribution paragraph for a future GEB cover letter

**Status:** editorial draft only; no submission, correspondence, author identity, priority claim or journal acceptance implied. This contribution statement is deliberately **separate from the scientific Results/Discussion**.

> We would like to submit our Research Article, “Global photographic phenomics reveals geographically structured precipitation–capitulum associations in *Cirsium*”, to *Global Ecology and Biogeography*. Although global functional-trait compilations span many taxa, categorical flower descriptions and single species means compress within- and among-taxon variation across climatic regions. The regional radiations and globally available public photographs of *Cirsium* provide an opportunity to quantify capitulum presentation, visible colour and shape as continuous traits at large scale. Across 168,907 eligible photographic observations from 178 native taxa, capitulum image angle was positively associated with warm-quarter precipitation in the original multiplicity-corrected model. Crucially, an independently executed **source-identical geographic sensitivity audit** recovered this coefficient and showed that it attenuated with fixed regional and spherical geographic controls and disappeared when eastern-sector taxa were held out. The study therefore distinguishes **a reproducible global trait–climate pattern from a geographically universal trait response** and makes regional composition a substantive ecological result, not a generic limitation. A complementary comparison of native and introduced ranges of two source-eligible species reveals three Holm-supported floral-colour gradients with radiation or elevation in introduced strata, without attributing differences in statistical support to new adaptation. The manuscript combines large-scale photographic phenotyping, transparent cohort provenance and biogeographically structured environmental associations, while retaining weak exploratory trait–trait correlations only in Supporting Information.
