**Strong gate:** ≥100 angle-assessable observations, ≥15 occupied 5° equal-sine-latitude cells and ≥10 observers *independently within each range*; the weaker gate (≥30 angle records, ≥5 cells, ≥10 observers in each range) is also satisfied only by these two species. The five others have only **2–32 introduced-angle-assessable records** and **1–4 introduced cells**, precluding comparably supported strata.

*Selection chronology:* *C. arvense* and *C. vulgare* were selected before the all-350 recheck. The 350-name audit subsequently confirmed that those candidates exhaust the species passing the same measured-support rules **in this source snapshot**. Thus “empirically defensible case selection” is justified; “fully prospective, random species selection” is not. The source audit declares that no measured **angle values** or **rainfall values** were used to select species, although photo availability/angle-assessability counts were used to determine eligibility.

**Source boundary:** original source observations were **665,115** (including other rank/status categories); only **156,750** observations were explicitly introduced wild in this source, of which **156,608 (99.91%)** came from the selected two species. This describes the **retrieved data**, not the population of all introduced thistles globally. WCVP flags are regional; introduced specimens are disproportionately from North America, and original source-status gaps may yield false negatives.

**No climate-niche analysis is conducted here.** BIO18, BIO05, RSDS and elevation remain predictors of photographed traits in the 24 October 2026 frozen 128-test S2 family. The audit explains **why these two species could be compared**; it does not estimate historic evolutionary rates or show genetic adaptation after introduction.
