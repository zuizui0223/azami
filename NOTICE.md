# Licensing scope

The root MIT license applies to original software and software documentation contributed by the Azami authors. It does not replace the licenses of third-party software, photographs, maps, environmental data, phylogenetic backbones or other externally supplied material. Preserve their original notices and attribution requirements.

Photograph rights remain with their respective rights holders under the recorded image-specific licenses. CHELSA, Natural Earth, WCVP/POWO, phylogenetic backbones and pretrained-model dependencies retain their own terms. Redistribution of a derived numerical package requires checking those terms separately; the presence of this MIT license does not by itself license every file in an external data archive.

Historical deposition metadata is retained as published. A new deposition should distinguish the software license from the data and image licenses instead of applying MIT indiscriminately to the whole package.
