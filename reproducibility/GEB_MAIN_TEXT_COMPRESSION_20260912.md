- Results 3.8–3.10, revision/conditional/bounding-box diagnostics: **~758 words**;

## New Main-text spine

### Introduction

1. Species/taxon summaries compress repeated phenotype information.
2. Cross-scale organization is not guaranteed: within-taxon covariance and among-taxon covariance answer different biological questions.
3. Public images provide repeated continuous measurements at global scale but carry trait-specific technical uncertainty.
4. Cirsium capitula provide multiple biologically interpretable constructs in one integrated floral structure.
5. Questions: recover constructs -> compare identical-cohort within/among geometry and strength -> place environmental associations in that organization -> test two retained ecological anchors through robustness gates.

### Results

1. Brief technical/data-domain context.
2. **Central result first:** modules persist but integration is stronger and geometrically reorganized among taxa.
3. Complete construct-environment context without row-by-row narration.
4. Two retained anchors and their robustness sequence.
5. WCVP taxonomy sensitivity confirms geometry/strength/anchors under accepted-name collapse.

### Discussion

1. Scale transition is the conceptual result: taxon medians are not simply averaged within-taxon phenotype organization.
2. The two ecological anchors occupy that reorganized phenotype but do not define one universal climate syndrome.
3. Chroma/radiation and presentation-angle/precipitation need different functional validation.
4. Public-image phenomics is useful because it retains repeated continuous variation while preserving measurement-specific uncertainty.
5. Generalization beyond Cirsium requires an independent lineage or direct developmental/genetic/functional measurement, not more models on the same matrix.

## Compression rules

- Do not remove a caveat if its corresponding claim remains in Main; shorten it or move the evidence/details to SI.
- Do not repeat the same limitation in Abstract, Results, Discussion and Conclusion unless it changes interpretation at each location.
- Do not narrate entire tables/heatmaps. State the pattern and use the display/archive for exhaustive values.
- Do not report every sensitivity coefficient in Main; report gate outcomes and the estimates needed for the headline.
- Keep denominators explicit: 46,276/259 is the global atlas; 1,734/42 is the primary direct-integration common cohort; 1,415/38 is the WCVP authority-remapped sensitivity cohort.
- Preserve the frozen primary source-assigned analysis as canonical; taxonomy remapping remains a sensitivity.
- Keep all post-hoc diagnostics labelled as such even after moving them to SI.
