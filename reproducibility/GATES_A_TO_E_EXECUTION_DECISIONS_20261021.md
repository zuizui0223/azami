## Gate C — density, overlap and shape of phenotypic distributions

- [Historical PR #92](https://github.com/zuizui0223/azami/pull/92) executed 50 observations × 30 repetitions, 90% KDE probability content, TPD overlap, Wasserstein W2 and bandwidth/same-species reference checks in a **different cohort** (54-taxon principal module analysis). This is a working methodology to reuse, not a result for the new 313,350-row source.
- A Gaussian 95% correlation ellipsoid in three image proxies is **not** a KDE-based density or functional hypervolume. Four colour fractions are compositional (sum to one), and circular hue is not two independent evolutionary traits.
- **Independently recomputed historical finite-sample diagnostic:** [source-locked Gate C verification](native_gate_c_existing_density.py), [actual PR92 result receipt](native_gate_c_pr92_reaudit_20261021.json) (original `6061bef`): among 33 orientation taxa, **109/528** between-taxon W2 pairs exceed their same-taxon p95 (only **13/528** after centroid removal); among 33 colour taxa, **466/528** (**349/528** centred); among 34 outline taxa, **77/561** (**47/561** centred). The original 4,318 intertaxon pair rows, 100 same-species reference taxa and 0.75/1/1.25 shared KDE bandwidth checks were audited against original Git blob hashes; this is a **46,276-observation full-range pilot**, not the new native-only Gate C result. Species pairs share taxa, overlap depends on dimension and bandwidth, and this is not a calibrated significance test.
- **Decision:** **BLOCKED for current native cohort** until Gate A support and Gate B same-frame weights/coordinates permit independently assessed n=20/50 and 0.75/1/1.25 bandwidth checks. Report probability-content volume, TPD overlap, full W2 and centred W2 separately.

The Gate D model selects **BIO1, BIO4, BIO12, BIO15 and RSDS** by its outcome-independent multilevel correlation/VIF contract, with no phenotype-dependent reselection. This five-variable conditional estimand is **not interchangeable** with other exploratory environment-model families.
- **Observed environmental signal:** the joint five-environment model has an **among-taxon orientation × annual rainfall** estimate of **+0.438 image-degrees per 100 mm** (95% species-cluster interval **+0.206 to +0.671**, Holm-adjusted **P=0.00531**); its within-taxon interval includes zero. Fixed coarse cell balancing preserves the positive between-taxon direction, not proof of spatial independence.
- **Historical placement sensitivity:** **52 alternative placement trees** cover **194 taxa** and **169,912** orientation observations; the between-taxon rainfall effect stays positive at **+0.4722 to +0.5078 degrees per 100 mm** across placements. All **104/104** have a phylogenetic covariance boundary. The estimator is **PSD-constrained quadratic moments**, not REML for this joint covariance stage, not genetic covariance, and not an unambiguous evolutionary module.
- **What still needs a distinct test:** shared-sample *environment × KDE/TPD/centred-Wasserstein* relationships, observer/camera/trait-eligibility selection, strict spatial-transfer tests and experimentally validated functions. These remain open **within PR #144's Gate D programme**; this PR #142 only tracks dependencies and keeps A/C/E separate.

## Gate E — independent evolutionary and ecological function evidence

- [EAzami PR #122](https://github.com/zuizui0223/EAzami/pull/122) is still **Draft/unmerged**: generative scale-decoupling structures fit some registered targets but remain provisional without independent held-out validation. They do not establish true genetic component independence.
- [aza3 PR #61](https://github.com/zuizui0223/aza3/pull/61) is **merged**: one published *C. arvense* block experiment shows a pollinator-exclusion minus control fertile-achene fraction difference **−0.523066** over **36/36 complete blocks**. This is a reproductive intervention, **not** a head-angle/colour/phyllary-specific functional effect.
- [aza3 PR #64](https://github.com/zuizui0223/aza3/pull/64) is **merged**: among **63** North American nuclear-tree tips, bird-coded vs insect-coded syndrome has outer-spreading phyllaries **6/7 vs 10/56**, with a conditional phylogenetic association. Original syndrome coding may partly incorporate floral traits; after removing red-corolla taxa only two bird-coded tips remain and the association is not identified. Do **not** use it as independent proof of bird delivery or evolutionary adaptation.
- **Falsifiable cross-gate alternatives, NOT established biological results:** (H1) global colour discrimination (historical Gate C) persists after native-only, taxon×observer×cell balancing and photo calibration; (H2) specific presentation×colour covariance candidates (historical Gate B 3/19 pointwise cross-module pairs) persist after pose correction, shared-pixel technical nulls and matched sampling rather than constituting a universal head syndrome; (H3) experimentally measured phyllary posture/access predicts independently observed pollinator contact or herbivore effects beyond syndromic labels and geography. Distinguish selection of extant combinations from correlated historical origins; the EAzami seven-taxon binary/continuous PCA and aza3 syndrome labels are **different evidence units** from the worldwide photographic observations.
- **Decision:** **CROSS-REPO EVIDENCE VERIFIED, EXACT-CONCEPT / INDIVIDUAL-LEVEL CROSSWALK NOT COMPLETE**. WCVP accepted keys, Japanese taxon concepts, geographic sampling, original pollinator/fitness interventions and phylogenetic tips must be linked at their actual evidence unit. No source-photo proxy becomes true gravity angle, pigment chemistry, exudate, spine length, defence or genetic LEGO without independent field measurements.
