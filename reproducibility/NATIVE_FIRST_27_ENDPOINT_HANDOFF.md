### Single question and scientific distinction

**Primary question:** *How do covariance, occupancy breadth, density overlap and ecological association of the independently measurable visible components of Cirsium capitula reorganize within taxa versus among taxa, after selecting the native-range source population before any phenotype-based screening?*

A mere increase in ellipsoid volume, a new visually persuasive PCA plot, or a collection of individually significant environmental slopes is **not** the target discovery. The central possibilities are:

- **Mean shift without reassembly:** environmental or lineage contrasts displace the head-phenotype-space centroid, leaving correlation structure and distribution shape similar.
- **Variance expansion or contraction:** axes broaden/narrow with comparable correlation structure; this changes dispersion without establishing new independent component combinations.
- **Covariance rotation / combination filtering:** the correlation geometry, orientation of principal covariance axes or attainable joint-phenotype combinations change, possibly without a change in marginal ranges or total volume.
- **Distribution turnover without simple mean change:** distinct densities occupy different subregions, although centroids agree. Compare probability overlap and transport distance, with same-taxon finite-sample baselines.
- **Scale reversal:** the above can differ among repeated observations inside a taxon versus contrasts of taxon means. They are separate descriptive estimands, not genetic or selective mechanisms.

The hierarchy of deliverables is **source/measurement support → matched covariance structure → supported phenotype probability distributions → predeclared ecological contrast → ancestry and function**. The last stage belongs to independent EAzami/aza3 evidence, not retrospective labelling of Azami pixels as biological fitness functions.

### One scientific workflow, with hard entry and stop gates

**Gate A — source, measurements, and comparability.** Use the pinned original **665,115** source observations and pre-phenotype **313,350** WCVP/TDWG3-native observations (306 accepted keys) as the source roster. Reuse existing image observations and climate caches only; the 217,104 inherited measurement rows and 96,246 unmatched records remain separate. Produce the **per-module and pairwise exact same-observation intersection**, a fully matched core-three-module cohort, and a hierarchy of usable taxon × spatial cell × photographer counts. Use equal-taxon/equal-region/equal-observer sensitivity and explicit QC failure/anthesis/camera-pose fields where actually present. **STOP** a module if measurement meaning, native linkage or independent technical QC is not defensible. An endpoint's missingness must not be coded as a biological zero.

**Gate B — shape of multivariate integration before volume.** On exactly the same admitted observations and shared, outcome-blind scaling, estimate within-taxon **W**, among-taxon **B**, and their exact weighted pooled identity **T=W+B**; output pairwise signed correlation, circular-hue joint multivariate association, module × module RV (with equal-n/permutation-baseline sensitivity), eigenvalue/eigenvector rotations and cross-scale matrix alignment. Report **different marginal variances, different covariance, and different centroid** separately. Use taxon/cell/photographer and image/observation cluster uncertainty as the observational sampling design permits. Form a null that preserves each trait's within-taxon marginal distribution and the correct taxon/photographer/region strata, but destroys its *joint pairing*, to evaluate whether unusual phenotypic combinations require more than marginal availability. **STOP** “recombination / LEGO / genetic constraint” claims if only JPEG covariance is measured.

**Gate C — recover real density-based distribution estimands.** Reuse [PR #92's distribution code](https://github.com/zuizui0223/azami/blob/6061befaf56ee545bb3b2b4a80ed220d2def1190/analysis/v3/run_distribution_breadth_pilot.py) and [its pairwise methods](https://github.com/zuizui0223/azami/blob/6061befaf56ee545bb3b2b4a80ed220d2def1190/analysis/v3/run_distribution_overlap_pilot.py) *after adapting source identity/assessability only*. First establish module-specific 1D/low-dimensional, shared scaled **TPD/KDE 90%-probability volume**, pairwise `integral min(p,q)` density overlap, exact empirical `W2`, and its centroid versus centred-distribution contribution. Fixed shared PCA bases must be estimated without using environmental associations or manually selecting attractive taxa, retain a fixed hue sine/cosine metric and disclose projection variance. Standardize sampling effort (PR #92 benchmark: 50/taxon × 30 draws, use outcome-blind available support rather than forcing 50 if native eligibility fails); calibrate same-taxon 50+50 or feasible matched reference and bandwidth sensitivity (0.75/1/1.25). **No “full capitulum hypervolume”** if the joined full-module cohort is too sparse or the shared projection discards biologically important variance. The present `native_space_gate.py` **Gaussian ellipsoid** is a quick feasibility/covariance proxy, *not a replacement* for this density layer.

**Gate D — predeclared environmental correspondence and validation.** Ask **three distinct questions**, in this order: (1) centroid shifts, (2) variation/volume/overlap and eigen-axis rotation, (3) replacement/restriction of *joint phenotype combinations*, rather than searching 27×9 slopes. Start with the already defined **BIO12 (precipitation) and RSDS (radiation)** questions for continuity; record climatic **mean**, within-range **heterogeneity** and occupied **geographic breadth** separately. Expand only to historically predefined thermal/hydric/radiative/mechanical/growing-season/resource blocks if support permits an outcome-independent comparison. Contrast **within-taxon same-taxon environmental strata** against **among-taxon environmental sorting**; keep a common support set for each contrast. Require spatial-block and held-out taxon/region prediction versus simple baselines, dominant-taxon omission, same-observer QC and multiple-comparison reporting before ecological generalization. The historical PR #92's KDE-breadth correlations (+0.424 orientation, −0.126 colour, +0.402 outline) are *hypothesis-generating nonnative-frame values*, and its out-of-region prediction increments were mostly weak/negative: **do not prefill any native outcome with their signs**.

**Cross-repository handoff only after these gates.** `azami` owns **visible phenotype covariance, density and present-day ecological correspondence**. `EAzami` asks whether the corresponding exact lineage concepts underwent separate/recurrent transformations and whether present-state ecological regimes characterize **origin versus maintenance**; lineage-specific sample coverage and trees cannot be inferred from global photo means. `aza3` evaluates *same-individual* anatomy, developmental/pigment mechanisms, ancestry/cytotype and **pollinator/antagonist/reproductive-fitness effects**, testing whether apparent phenotypic modules are functional/genetic rather than photographic or mathematical. No historical or functional evolution is "confirmed" by a large observational hypervolume.

Comparisons use one observation per taxon × 0.5-degree cell and equally weighted accepted taxa.
