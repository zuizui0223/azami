# Native prediction failure diagnostic
The 256-cell rarefaction and fixed kNN approach failed every native holdout. Test whether additional native training coverage or simpler regularization addresses this, without using introduced outcomes. Leave out each native 20-degree block with at least eight eligible cells; require at least 100 training cells and eight held-out blocks. Use original eight endpoints, identical record-equalcell feature definition and fold-specific native references. Compare fixed kNN20, kNN100, ridge alpha .1*n and ridge alpha n on five training-standardized annual climate axes. CDF predictions clipped to [0,1]; hue Fourier features not clipped. Baseline is training mean feature vector. Report total SSE skill, block-equal relative skill, number of positive blocks, min/max block skill, approximate whole-block sign sensitivity, Holm across 64 planned comparisons, unqualified P=1. LOBO training sets overlap; significance is only exploratory. Diagnostic pass requires positive pooled and equal-block skill, at least half blocks positive and Holm<.05. Do not choose a model on introduced effects. No further tuning if none passes; even a pass requires independent validation. No genetic mechanism inferred.
