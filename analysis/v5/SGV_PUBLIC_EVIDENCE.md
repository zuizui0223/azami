# SGV rapid adaptation: public evidence and next data gates
Reviewed 2026-11-26.

- Battlay et al. 2025, Nature Ecology & Evolution, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-025-02751-2. White clover: 2,660 individuals, standing haploblocks, parallel climate adaptation, four field common gardens. Direct methodological benchmark, not evidence for Cirsium. Dryad https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.dfn2z3593 contains common-garden fitness data for 472 individuals.
- Hodgins et al. 2020, Plant Communications, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xplc.2020.100116. Native European vs introduced North American C. arvense common gardens and stress treatments, genetically based quantitative trait differentiation; no focal head colour evidence. Guggisberg et al. 2012 https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2699.2012.02746.x invasion history; Guggisberg et al. 2013 https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.12258 transcriptome divergence. Multiple introduction history requires ancestry-aware comparisons.
- Bures et al. 2024, Plant Biology, https://doi.org/10.1111/plb.13653. C. vulgare intergeneric hybrid origin; RADseq of 8 focal samples plus 23 other representatives; public BioProject PRJNA1032292 https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1032292 (31 experiments, 31 BioSamples). These are species-origin data, not yet a geographically replicated population selection panel. Allotetraploidy and homeolog mapping matter for any future allele-frequency work.
- C. vulgare NCBI taxonomy 92907 https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/435298 (RefSeq umbrella with no linked public data on that page; not proof of a usable reference). C. arvense 41550 https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/1156065 (female plant genome umbrella, not population data).
- Introduction history: USDA FEIS https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/species-reviews/cirvul (eastern N America colonial times; scattered western introductions late 1800s/early 1900s). NZPCN https://www.nzpcn.org.nz/flora/species/cirsium-vulgare/ (naturalised 1867); NIES https://www.nies.go.jp/biodiversity/invasive/DB/detail/80470e.html (Japan Hokkaido first record 1960s, North American origin); Chang & Tseng 2020, Q J Forest Res 42:49–58, Taiwan newly naturalized report https://www.researchgate.net/publication/340787314_Cirsium_vulgare_Compositae_a_newly_naturalized_plant_in_Taiwan (voucher Chang 2523, TNM/TCF, figures; not proven introduction date); https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12899020/ Yunnan first recognized 2022 (not introduction date). New populations provide potential prospective natural experiments; neither age nor geographic contrasts alone are evidence of temporal evolution.
- Public archive discovery must count independent biosamples, not sequence runs. No genotype-phenotype join or location-based inference of ancestry from metadata. A single genome, taxonomic phylogeny, plastid barcode, pooled transcriptome or RNA-seq experiment is not an adequate population-genomic selection design. First audit method, sampling country/date and sample duplication; inspect original study design and metadata for comparable native/introduced sampling and matched phenotypes. No SRA download before this gate.
