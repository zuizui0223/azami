# Climate overlap versus directional occupancy excess

A second exploratory specification, not a replacement chosen by significance. For normalized availability-corrected occupancy densities N and I, min(N,I) is shared density, max(I-N,0) is introduced excess density, and their masses sum to 1. Excess mass = 1-Schoener D. This is not a 95% native-core definition and is not necessarily outside native environmental support.

- Assign each original 1-degree complete-case cell to its original median PCA bin. A cell's signed score (I-N)/(I+N) distinguishes introduced-enriched from native-enriched climate. Require positive introduced density and report coverage; no extrapolation.
- Quantify all three native-reference trait distributions and joint bins with common/excess climate mass weights (one target mass per bin split equally across observed cells); report supported target mass and conditional re-normalization. The same cell may contribute to both components; these are descriptive weighted mixtures, not independent groups.
- Independent exploratory inference: geographic one-to-one pairing of positive and nonpositive score introduced cells within 20-degree blocks using frozen V5 matching; whole-block paired swaps with Holm as in first wave. 'analogue'/'novel' column names in reused code mean nonpositive/positive score here, not 95% contour. Continuous within-region score slopes for native tail frequencies, mean and absolute deviation from native median, with cluster t intervals, block sign score sensitivity, leave-one-block-out, Holm across all slopes within representation. Conditional on fixed estimated density and contemporary native reference; geographic blocks are approximations, no inference of genetic mechanism.
- No original observations/coordinates/observer identifiers exported; no updates to main. Both waves use original three traits only, including outline as photographic index. Synthetic unit tests verify algebra and code, not biological outcomes.
