# Existing versus native-unobserved phenotype variation
Direct contrast requested by user; keep cross-sectional three-group logic and omit all temporal/genetic/predictive models. Fixed V5 95% native-climate contour (primary) and density surplus (sensitivity), matched pairs, original complete-case cells, equal total weight per cell and usable-head-gated archived observations.

Two distinct quantities: (1) frequency in bins unobserved in the reference native sample, (2) positive frequency change from analogous to novel climate partitioned into native-observed bins and native-unobserved bins. Their sum is TV; for the second quantity report the fraction of redistributed mass associated with existing bins (undefined if no redistribution). Also report the same partition from the native reference to introduced novel. These are descriptive distribution quantities; a 100% existing share can occur when bins saturate and is not evidence of selection.

Full reference uses all available native observations. Scalar traits: five native quintile intervals with explicit below-minimum and above-maximum classes; hue: 12 fixed circular sectors. The seven linear traits and hue separately, plus C*/L* and C*/L*/angle joint spaces with the identical grids, no candidate selected by its p. Native-unoccupied bins include novel combinations within marginal bounds. All are observed-space bins, not new mutations. The full-reference native unobserved frequency is zero by construction.

Balanced reference sensitivity: 32 geographically disjoint native splits, 256 reference cells, 128 native calibration cells, one random eligible observation per cell (so identical reference sample counts across both species). Each draw fits only its native reference bins and scores held-out native calibration observations and both introduced matched groups against the same reference. Average per-cell unobserved mass and distribution metrics across draws; not independent replicates. This measures sensitivity to finite native sampling: the held-out native rate shows how often native observations would be classified as unseen. It is not a proof of equivalent phenotype support. Native-vs-introduced baseline comparisons are descriptive; inferential contrast is within introduced matched regions, whole-block paired swaps on average unobserved mass. Conditional pointwise block bootstrap intervals. Holm all 80 planned tests and 20 per reference/definition family; noneligible comparisons p=1. No inference of genetic novelty, ancestry, evolutionary rates, fitness, or selection. Photo measures are observation-level medians and may not correspond to the same individual head. No further model search.
