## Adopted interpretation and next figure

The main new finding is coexistence of broad native-observed phenotype support in climate-novel settings and a modest positive spatial association between climatic and phenotypic support-distance in C. vulgare, not significant geometric turnover/expansion. This directly complements earlier 95%-definition chroma concentration (Holm24=.0264; sensitivity-dependent) and similar-climate range contrast (Holm12=.00284). It motivates a standing-phenotype redistribution hypothesis but does not demonstrate preferential recruitment of standing genetic variants. Keep earlier failed native-prediction, overlap, species contrast and dispersion tests visible in SI. No edits to poster/V6 manuscript yet.
