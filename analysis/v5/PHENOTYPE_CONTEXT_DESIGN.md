# Third wave: coverage and climatic context (exploratory)

Two separate aims: (1) quantify the sensitivity of mass-weighted phenotype summaries to sparse fine-grid support by aggregating original fine-grid min(N,I) and max(I-N,0) masses to 2x2, 4x4, 8x8 bins, keeping the original definition of common/excess mass fixed. Report supported mass and effective number of cells; coarsening assumes cells represent their coarse bin, does not fill missing values or turn descriptive weighted mixtures into independent samples. (2) Identify candidate climate axes for differences between geographically matched introduced cells. For both 95% native core and density-surplus definitions, fit paired phenotype contrasts on five jointly entered climate contrasts scaled by native SD; test all three traits and mean/upper/lower tail outcomes, median and record-equalcell representations. Include date and number-of-records difference sensitivity. Pointwise clustered t intervals, block score sign sensitivity and leave-one-block-out ranges; Holm within each definition/representation/adjustment family, undefined P values counted conservatively. This is an exploratory model on matched pairs, not a causal decomposition, and its score test is approximate. No private records or location identifiers exported. The sign of the effect for any climate axis is not predefined. Climate group selection itself uses climate variables, so their differences are descriptive, not independent tests of climate novelty. No expansion beyond the original three traits at this stage.
