# Native-reference redistribution in non-overlapping climate space (exploratory, 2026-11-26)

Question: Does expansion into climatic space not commonly occupied by the present-day native range accompany reweighting of existing phenotype variation, or shifts beyond its observed support? Do not assume dryness, higher chroma or any direction beforehand.

- Two focal species, exact V5 confidential source and five annual CHELSA climate variables; preserve original deduplication, WCVP range definitions and frozen climate D. Primary 95% native occupied climate contour; 90/99% sensitivity. The outside contour is not identical to the strict support complement or 1-D.
- Main unit: 1-degree cell; equal cell weights. Match introduced analogue and novel cells within 20-degree geographic blocks as V5. Native reference fixed; compare distributions relative to native quantiles without fitting them to introduced observations. Record-equalcell representation is a sensitivity that retains within-cell variation, not a count of independent individuals.
- All original three traits and 2D/3D combinations (outline remains a projected image index). Seven fixed native-reference bins, tail frequencies within observed native range, strict observed extrema exceedance, mean, and combinations of native tertiles. Ties may create empty bins. Rare existing joint category = positive native frequency <=2%; unobserved category is not proof of a new genetic variant. Climate opportunity classes retained as descriptive sensitivity. 
- Two-sided whole-block paired label swaps (exact up to 15 blocks, Monte Carlo otherwise); Holm across species/traits/categories within each predeclared family, coverage and representation. Pointwise paired-block percentile intervals hold native reference fixed. Omnibus total variation detects any frequency difference; signed departure from native compares distances to the same native reference. No testing on private identifiers, no publication of raw data.
- Interpret as exploratory because V5 results were already examined. Even a robust frequency shift cannot distinguish selection on standing genetic variation, source sorting, founder effects, plasticity, demographic history, or image measurement artifacts. Native current distribution is a reference, not ancestral state. A lack of observed out-of-support values is not proof of no novelty.
- This first wave uses the original three-trait complete cases only. Additional lightness/hue or image features need source and quality checks and a distinct declared exploratory family.
