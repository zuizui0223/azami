# Native-only climate/phenotype transfer (exploratory)

Question: can present-day native climate-associated variation account for the observed change of phenotype distribution in introduced climatic surplus/novel climates? This is a conditional prediction diagnostic, not an evolutionary counterfactual.

- All eight archived endpoints, equal cell total weights for eligible records, frozen 3-trait-complete cells and original geographic pairs; 95% native climate contour primary, density surplus sensitivity. Native reference in each draw is fixed before testing introduced outcomes.
- Thirty-two fixed native geographic block splits, 256 reference cells and 128 geographically held-out native cells. Fit a fixed 20-nearest-neighbor conditional distribution estimator on the five native-standardized annual climate axes, no outcome-based tuning. Scalar outcomes use 19 empirical CDF features on within-training native ranks, circular hue uses three Fourier moment pairs. Predict by averaging eligible reference-cell feature vectors. Evaluate held-out native Q² against the training mean and fraction of positive validation draws; require pooled Q²>0 and at least half the valid draws positive for any inferential claim. Rank thresholds are fitted on training records only. 
- A target is within the native climate support when its mean 20-neighbor climate distance is within the 95th percentile of geographic native validation distances in that draw; use only pairs with both sides supported in >=80% of all 32 draws. Report coverage and supported pairs, no extrapolation. For each pair average observed and predicted changes over identical supported draws; do not count draws as independent.
- Compare observed versus native-predicted distribution differences, residual RMS and alignment, plus signed mean-rank and existing-tail frequency contrasts. Only if native prediction validates and >=20 pairs, >=8 blocks, >=50% pair retention, perform approximate whole-block sign inference on the residual contrast. Holm across the predeclared 16 endpoints/species within each novelty definition; nonqualified endpoints stay in family p=1 and are not evidence of equality. Inference conditional on estimated native models and fixed support, not a randomized treatment.
- Good prediction is compatible with conserved present-day native phenotype–climate association and ecological fitting; residual changes could arise from source sorting, altered environment relationships, plasticity, drift, measurement bias or selection. No proof of fitness, inheritance or allele-frequency change, and no claim that current native records are pre-introduction ancestors. Novel climates outside support remain unidentifiable in this design. Only aggregate outputs.
